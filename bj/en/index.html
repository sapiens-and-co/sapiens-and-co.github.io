What you can do

Play, learn, test yourself

Hit, Stay, Double, Split, Surrender. Learn which one is best in every spot, right at the table.

Play

Know if every decision was the best play

Bet, deal, decide, settle. Play on a table that runs just like the real game, and the grading box shows the right play for each hand.

Grading can be Off, On (Simple) or On (Pro). On (Pro) also shows the EV (%) of every action.

Strategy Chart

A chart for your table's rules

Hard, Soft and Pairs. Open it with one tap during a game whenever you are unsure.

Quick Drill · Skill Test

Drill it, then test it

Quick Drill deals only the first decision, over and over. Skill Test plays 10, 25 or 50 hands to the end and shows your accuracy.

In the drill, choose the hand type and the dealer's upcard. Test results show your accuracy for Hard, Soft and Pairs.

Best Play Navi

A quick check when you’re unsure

Pick your cards and the dealer's upcard, and it tells you the best play for that spot.

Table rules

Different rules, different best plays

Set the rules to match the table you will play, and the grading and the Strategy Chart change to the best plays for that table.

  • Game type (Standard blackjack / Free Bet Blackjack)
  • Blackjack pays (3:2 or 6:5)
  • Dealer soft 17
  • Decks (1–8)
  • Dealer hole card
  • Surrender
  • Double down on
  • Double after split (DAS)
  • Split up to

We do not collect personal information

BJ Trainer needs no account and keeps your play data on your device. Money changes hands only when you buy Pro, and that goes through an outside payment service.

No sign-up

There is no name or email to register and no login. The app does not collect personal information.

Your play data stays on your device

Settings, Lite counts and language are saved only in your device's browser. The app never sends what you play (hands or results) to the operator. To improve the app, it records only how often it is opened and how long it is used, in a way that does not identify you.

Payments through Paddle

You buy Pro on the checkout of Paddle, a separate company from the operator. Paddle is the seller of record: it takes the payment and handles sales tax. Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Paddle and never reach the operator.

About Paddle

Questions

FAQ

Do I play with real money?
No. The app uses play money only. There is no real-money betting, no prizes and no cash-out.
Is it on the App Store or Google Play?
No. BJ Trainer is a web app that runs in your phone's browser. Add it to your Home Screen and it works just like an app. We'll consider the stores depending on how people respond, but there are no plans for now. If we release a store version in the future, we plan to let you carry over Pro with the email address you used to buy it, so you won't need to buy it again.
What happens to Pro if I change phones?
We plan to let you bring it back with “Restore purchase”. We will share the steps once they are ready.
Do I need an internet connection?
Only the first time. Once opened, most features work even offline.
Will learning basic strategy make me win?
No guarantee. Basic strategy is a way of playing that keeps the house edge (the built-in advantage of the dealer side) as small as possible.
