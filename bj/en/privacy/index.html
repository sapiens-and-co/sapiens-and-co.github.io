Privacy Policy

Sapiens & Co. (the “Operator”) handles information in “BJ Trainer” (the “App”) and its product page as described below.

1. Information we collect

The App has no account registration and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

2. Information saved on your device

Settings, Lite counts, language and similar data are saved only in your device’s browser storage. They are never sent to the Operator’s servers. Clearing your browser’s site data also deletes this information.

3. Usage records and advertising

To improve the App, it records only how often it is opened and how long it is used, in a way that does not identify you. It never sends what you play (hands or results), your name or your email address. We do not use outside analytics tools or advertising tags.

4. Delivery logs

The App is delivered by Cloudflare and the product page by GitHub Pages. To deliver them, access logs (such as IP addresses and browser types) may be kept by each company.

5. Buying Pro

When you buy Pro, a payment service run by a separate company from the Operator handles your payment details, email address and related information. Card numbers and other payment details never reach the Operator. When sales start, this section will name the payment service and link to its privacy policy.

6. Contact form

  • What you enter in the contact form, including your email address, is used only to reply to you.
  • The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated submissions. Information needed for this is sent to Cloudflare.

7. Changes to this policy

Changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact form.
